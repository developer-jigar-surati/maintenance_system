## Brand Voice Guidelines

### Tone

- **Friendly** - Not corporate or stuffy
- **Encouraging** - Supportive, not judgmental about money
- **Simple** - No jargon, easy to understand
- **Trustworthy** - Reliable, secure, professional
- **Empowering** - You can do this, we're here to help

### Do's

- Use "you" and "your" (personal)
- Keep sentences short
- Use numbers and specifics
- Include calls-to-action
- Be relatable about money struggles

### Don'ts

- Don't use complex financial jargon
- Don't shame people about spending
- Don't make unrealistic promises
- Don't be pushy or salesy
- Don't ignore Indian context (use ₹, reference local situations)

### Example Captions

**Good:**
> "That ₹200 coffee habit? It adds up to ₹6,000 a month. 😱 Not saying stop - just know where your money goes. Track it free with Hisab Journey. Link in bio! ☕💰"

**Bad:**
> "Utilize our comprehensive financial management solution to optimize your expenditure allocation and maximize savings potential."
